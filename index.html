My Music

Your songs live in Telegram. This player streams them from your own bot — no storage used on your phone.

  1. Open Telegram and message @BotFather → send /newbot and follow the steps. Copy the token it gives you.
  2. Open your new bot and send it your songs as audio files or music files.
  3. Paste the token below and tap Save, then Sync.

Notes: the token stays in this browser only — keep it on a private device, or revoke it via @BotFather. Sync within 24 hours of sending songs. Files over 20 MB can't be streamed via the bot API. Don't set a webhook on the bot.
